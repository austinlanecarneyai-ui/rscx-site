Privacy policy

RS Classic Continuum is a small private RuneScape Classic server run by The RS Classic Continuum Team. This page says what we keep about you and why.

What we keep

  • Your character's name, and its password - stored only as a one-way hash we cannot read back.
  • Your email address, if you give one, and whether you confirmed it.
  • The internet address you signed up from and last logged in from.
  • Your game data: levels, items, quests and where you are in the world.
  • Whether you are a subscriber, and until when.
  • If you subscribe: your payments (date, amount, whether they went through, refunds), the country of your card and of your billing address, the card type and its last four digits, your renewal settings, and the time you agreed to the Terms and asked your month to start at once.
  • If you lodge a support ticket: what you wrote, our replies, its category, and the payment it names, if any.
  • Your inbox: messages about your subscription, payments, tickets and outages. Each is deleted 14 days after it arrives, or sooner if you delete it.

We never see or keep your card number. Payments are taken by Stripe on a page Stripe runs.

What we use it for

  • To run the game and keep your character.
  • To send you account emails: confirming your address and resetting your password. Nothing else - no newsletters, no marketing.
  • To answer your support tickets, and to look into a payment or a player you report.
  • To stop abuse, such as too many sign-ups or password guesses from one address.
  • To take your subscription payments, tell you when one fails, and keep the tax records the law requires. Your card's country decides the VAT included in the price.

Who else sees it

We never sell or share your data. It is stored with the services that host the game: Amazon Web Services (the game server and its email), GitHub (this website and the downloads) and Cloudflare (the domain name). They handle it only to provide those services.

Payments are processed by Stripe, which receives your card details, billing address and email directly from you and keeps them under its own privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy. We send Stripe only your character's number and, if you confirmed one, your email address, so it can send you receipts.

Cookies

None for tracking or advertising. Signing in stores a sign-in token in your browser; signing out removes it.

Age

You must be 13 or older to create an account.

Deleting your data

Payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires (up to ten years for EU VAT), even if you stop subscribing.

Accounts and characters are not deleted. You can change your email address at any time on My account.

Contact

support@rsclassic-continuum.com. This policy is written for Australia: the Privacy Act 1988 and its Australian Privacy Principles.
